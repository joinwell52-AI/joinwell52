A1 · First experiment candidate: settle unknown outcomes before retrying ​

Based on: O2、O7、O8、O9、O10、O11、O15、O16、O19、O37。

Supported: independent projects distinguish acceptance, launch, persistence, delivery and ownership, but their scoped fixes do not establish exactly-once. Hypothesis: CodeFlowMu recovery across PM/workers, host restart or model changes may need independently checkable effects before retry, without a demonstrated local defect. Candidate: an isolated fake write tool with occurrence IDs; cut connectivity after acceptance, after effect and before receipt, restart/change workers, and inspect counts/ownership/delivery through an independent reader. Require no duplicate effect, honest unknown outcomes, no stale artifact overwrite and no writes from fenced owners. Costs include durable receipts, fencing and provider queries; unqueryable effects remain unknown. Compare FCoP task/artifact/evidence correlation separately from Runtime leases/cancellation. Proposed, not run.
