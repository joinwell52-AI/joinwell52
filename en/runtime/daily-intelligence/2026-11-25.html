Research analysis ​

A1 · Priority experiment: which task, SOP and grant survives handoff? ​

Evidence：O4、O5、O6、O25、O35、O36。

Evidence: parent evidence, durable cutoffs, current question IDs and admission-time policy constrain upstream decisions; StaffDeck separates SOP requirements from grants. A separate Nov 24 controlled CodeFlowMu/FCoP 4.0.5 study recorded 12 executions, 24 review judgments (12 formal reviews), one missing-source control, and a two-ticket migration pair: only the approved ticket adopted V2; neither repeated bank verification, and only that ticket repeated waiver execution/review once. These synthetic program-interpreted controls do not establish autonomous reasoning. Highest-value next trial: real worker/reviewer agents under version changes, pending/effective decisions, revocation, missing originals and shift identity changes. Requires retrievable originals and scoped decisions; costs include version retention and independent review. FCoP concerns traceable references/decisions/evidence; enforcement and cache invalidation belong to runtime/adapters. High-priority unrun experiment; engagement: invalidating resumed approval after evidence changes.

A2 · Priority experiment: recover delivery or repeat an unknown action? ​

Evidence：O8、O14、O18、O20、O29、O31、O34。

Evidence: MCP 404 recovery avoids replay, A2A sends once with unknown outcomes, and Orca gates empty-answer retries on effect evidence; Paperclip distinguishes accepted-result delivery loss from repeated blocked-cycle wakes. Inference: stable IDs, backoff, receipts and artifact retrieval must be combined; one idempotency key or successful run does not prove delivery. Candidate: count external effects under lost responses, crashes around report persistence, new/same attempts, deterministic dependency failure and revocation. Compare external executions, accepted results, delivery and acceptance separately. FCoP concerns attempt/report/receipt/unknown-result semantics; retry policy remains runtime. Requires independent external truth and fault injection; carries historical work, unrun. Engagement: querying an already-applied effect without resending it.

A3 · Research actor identity, credentials and delegation limits separately ​

Evidence：O3、O12、O13、O15、O16、O30、O37、O43。

Evidence separates session owner, allowed accounts, specialist routing and native child grants. OPEN community issue #6215 supplies a specific unchanged-bearer claim, not a universal finding. Quota-account change affects liveness without establishing task authority. Candidate: compare shared/per-specialist/exchanged tokens and revocation across tenants, accounts and same-name specialists; test concurrent shared-budget reservation, failed attempts and resume. OPEN #6211/#6212 remain design references. Requires identity/tool-service cooperation and token/audit costs. FCoP can compare delegation/responsibility evidence; credential issuance, pre-call authorization and rate limits belong to runtime/gateway. Unrun candidate/ongoing observation. Engagement: distinguish verifiable acting agent from represented user.

A4 · Test long-run liveness, Stop and evidence delivery independently ​
