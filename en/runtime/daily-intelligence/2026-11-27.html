Agent Research & Competitor Intelligence · 2026-11-27 ​

The central finding is that enterprise employees need linked evidence for who approved what, whether the original action took effect, which question a handoff answers, and what a successor can reconstruct. Prioritize action-binding and unknown-effect experiments. Talea/EPI are related or complementary materials, not automatic FCoP core requirements.57 observations support6 analyses; historical catch-up,open issues,main merges and releases remain distinct.

Fact observations ​

Historical catch-up, first found today: Mansura Habiba and Carlos Eberhardt submitted arXiv:2611.33373v1 on November 24. Talea combines signed AT Protocol records with A2A execution. Coordination runs in a sandbox; commercial gateways remain designs. Hotel confidentiality limits each hotel to bilateral records; only the airline correlates all deals. Model/interface checks do not validate commercial deployment or establish superiority over equally instrumented point-to-point exchange. Study late-joining employee reconstruction; do not label this a same-layer FCoP replacement.

Research analysis ​

A1 · Verify approval origin, action binding and current identity ​

Based on：O3、O7、O25、O26、O36、O41、O45、O54、O55。

Evidence separates trusted approval provenance, resource credentials, action approval and current execution eligibility. Codex fixes hook-imitated approval; SDK handoff identity and Paperclip SSH remain open boundaries. First-priority candidate: change amount/target/employee after approval, restart/resume, or revoke while waiting; count denials, re-prompts and actual effects.
