Research analysis ​

A1 · First priority: test revocation, receipt binding and actual effects ​

Based on O3、O9、O13、O17、O34、O49。

Evidence: empty lists and unknown receipts do not establish absence of effects; yesterday's live-agent/synthetic-business study includes a duplicate order. ARQUÍA and Rodeo offer negative controls, not CodeFlowMu validation. Hypothesis: authority changes between dispatch and commit, or a successor reuses a receipt belonging to another request/attempt. Candidate: hold model, prompt, service and ledger constant while varying revocation timing, request/attempt/amount binding, late cancellations and concurrent successors. Count active effects, missing writes, bad reuse, retained unknowns and writes after denial. FCoP concerns decision/receipt/evidence references; call-time enforcement, idempotency, authoritative lookup and compensation belong to runtime/connectors. This is a first-priority candidate requiring controlled services and clear commit boundaries. Engagement: distinguish evidence of detecting a revoked effect from preventing it.

A2 · Second priority: keep answers, acknowledgments and identity in scope ​

Based on O11、O14、O30、O35、O36、O38、O39、O43。

Evidence: Orca's conditional first-answer claim, sentThrough-bounded ACKs, peerconv namespace and carried askers repair concrete coordination errors; OpenHands shows recoverable batch ordering is also a contract. Hypothesis: FCoP relationships must remain attributable under reordering, duplicates, late answers, old ACKs and cross-host/user changes. Candidate controls: opposite concurrent reviews, late old-attempt receipts, old-page ACKs racing new sends, foreign dispatch IDs and changed requesters. Require only legitimate state transitions, no lost/misattributed reports and no ACK of unsent items. Database retry does not authorize business-effect replay. Compare protocol semantics separately from runtime storage/scheduling. Cost: deterministic message scheduling and fault injection. Engagement: does idempotent same-answer replay also avoid a second task-ready transition?

A3 · Third priority: prove pause, restart and delivery states ​

Based on O3、O16、O17、O25、O33、O34、O37、O40、O43、O44、O47。

Evidence includes same-session resume, run-message recovery, versioned daemon capabilities, actual restart impact and bounded alerts.
