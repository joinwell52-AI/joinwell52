# Community Edition staging

This directory is the optional second output layer of Editorial Architecture V2. The complete evidence-bearing parent remains the Research Center Edition in `staging/publication-candidates/` and, after release, the bilingual Research Center article paths.

A Community Edition is created only when a named professional community has a useful discussion angle. It must record:

- parent Research Center candidate ID and path;
- target community;
- new title and selected question;
- evidence subset retained from the parent;
- concrete engineering or architectural significance;
- discussion question;
- target-language path and release authorization.

It must not be a full copy, generic summary, or advertisement. TMPA, FCoP, CodeFlowMu, or another first-party project may appear only as a relevant research object, case, or evidence.

Recommended draft names:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD-parent-slug.community-name.en.md
YYYY-MM-DD-parent-slug.community-name.zh.md
```

Community-specific publishing skills may transform an authorized draft for Cursor, OpenAI Developer Community, LangGraph, CrewAI, OpenHands, Hugging Face, or another declared professional surface. Publication of the parent does not automatically authorize or require community distribution.
