# About Digital Employee Works / 关于数字员工工场

Digital Employee Works is maintained by **Zhu Wei / 朱卫 (joinwell52-AI)** as an independent, engineering-driven production project.

数字员工工场由 **朱卫（joinwell52-AI）** 作为独立、工程驱动的生产项目持续维护。

## Current capability / 当前能力

- Research Report Production Engine V1.3 / 研究报告生产机 V1.3
- Research Runtime Center V4
- Research Runtime Scheduler V2.0
- Research Skills V2.0
- Research Intelligence System V1.0

The product is a ChatGPT-based Research Analyst Digital Employee with three intelligence pipelines, three permanent research columns, complete-report production at 15:00, release-only Publication at 20:00, and GitHub-verifiable work records.

该产品是一个通过 ChatGPT 实现的 Research Analyst 数字员工，具备三条研究情报管线、三个长期研究栏目、15:00 完整报告生产、20:00 只负责发版，以及可通过 GitHub 验证的工作记录。

网站能力区中，TMPA 与 FCoP 位于第一排；CodeFlowMu 位于第二排首位，随后是 Digital Employee。

## Research orientation / 研究方向

- Digital Employee positions, workflows, runtime, governance, and evaluation;
- major AI-platform architecture and product evolution;
- open-source agent runtime, protocol, SDK, recovery, evaluation, and observability;
- AI work data and governance;
- formal multi-agent coordination;
- SME-first AI deployment.

## Method / 方法

```text
AI Platform + GitHub + Published Research intelligence
→ Three-column triage
→ Governed research lifecycle
→ Complete bilingual Publication Candidate
→ GitHub + website release
→ Runtime Record + Commit Verify
```

Observation Notes record external developments and working judgments. They do not automatically become evidence for the TMPA paper.

观察笔记记录外部动态与工作判断，但不自动构成 TMPA 论文证据。
