## Decision

The `joinwell52-AI/joinwell52` repository is the public home of joinwell52 Research Center.

## Public role

The Portal publishes:

- TMPA papers and specifications;
- daily and weekly research;
- Digital Employee governing architecture;
- industry and open-source engineering analysis;
- future preprints, DOI records and formal publication status.

## Language architecture

English is the default Portal language. Chinese content lives under `/zh/`. English and Chinese articles are separate files and each paired article provides a language switch.

## Information hierarchy

```text
Research Programs
  TMPA · FCoP · CodeFlowMu · Digital Employee

Research Cadence
  Daily · Weekly · Academic Paper Watch

Research Domains
  Digital Employees · Industry · Engineering

Publications
  Papers · Specifications · Implementation Cases
```

## Technical architecture

```text
Versioned Markdown and Vue components
        ↓
VitePress
        ↓
GitHub Actions
        ↓
GitHub Pages
        ↓
joinwell52 Research Center Portal
```

## Editorial boundary

The public repository contains curated research outputs. Confidential implementation discussions, business-sensitive information, credentials and unreviewed evidence remain outside the public Portal.
