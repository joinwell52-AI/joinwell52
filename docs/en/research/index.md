# Research Center

TMPA Research Center operates as a continuous public research pipeline rather than a document archive.

## Research hierarchy

### Research cadence

- **Daily Research** — new papers, products, repositories, evidence, and decisions;
- **Weekly Synthesis** — cross-source analysis and explicit architectural judgment;
- **Publications** — stable papers, specifications, and implementation reports.

### Research domains

- **Academic Papers** — Agent systems, governance, protocols, evaluation, and AI work;
- **Digital Employees** — Position, responsibility, skills, workflow, runtime, governance, and evaluation;
- **Industry Architecture** — enterprise workforce and control-plane products;
- **Open-source Engineering** — runtime, workflow, recovery, observability, tools, and multi-Agent systems.

```text
Observation → Synthesis → Architecture → Engineering Evidence → Publication
```
