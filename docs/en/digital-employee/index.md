# Digital Employee Research

A Digital Employee is a persistent, software-defined organizational work unit represented externally by a **Position** and executed internally by a **managed work team**.

## Research structure

1. **Position and Responsibility** — purpose, scope, ownership, authority, and prohibited work;
2. **Skills and Tools** — reusable capabilities and governed access;
3. **Workflow and Runtime** — WorkOrder, planning, operation nodes, sessions, tools, recovery, and completion;
4. **Governance and Evaluation** — evidence, verification, cost, human authority, lifecycle, and improvement;
5. **Implementation** — CodeFlowMu as the Digital Employee development and work runtime.
