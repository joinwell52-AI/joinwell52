# Agent Research & Competitor Intelligence · 2026-11-27

The central finding is that enterprise employees need linked evidence for who approved what, whether the original action took effect, which question a handoff answers, and what a successor can reconstruct. Prioritize action-binding and unknown-effect experiments. Talea/EPI are related or complementary materials, not automatic FCoP core requirements.57 observations support6 analyses; historical catch-up,open issues,main merges and releases remain distinct.

## Fact observations

### O1 · Talea binds records to commitments, authority and resources; comparative benefit remains open

Model/interface checks do not validate commercial deployment or establish superiority over equally instrumented point-to-point exchange.
