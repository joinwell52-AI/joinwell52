### A2 · Recovery needs authoritative outcomes and durable pauses

O1、O5、O7、O9、O12、O17、O19、O26、O29

Evidence: commit/receipt/pause/retry fail in different windows; Paperclip provides concrete queued-brake and unknown-receipt cases. Unverified: mapping FCoP artifact receipts to Runtime settlement might reduce duplicate effects or permanent fences. Candidate tests: disconnect after commit, crash after pause during model I/O, and reordered acknowledgments. Unknown outcomes cannot automatically mean failure.

### A3 · Compare protocols along the actual connection chain

O2、O4、O6、O10、O11、O21、O25

MCP PR #3541 merges the six-month cycle announcement with a 2027-03-30 target, not a shipped specification. Experience candidates: PRP contracts and MCP Skills after version/license/dependency checks; potentially complementary to FCoP artifact coordination, with no evidence to replace its core.

### A4 · Acceptance needs governance and independent evidence

Anthropic recommends grader calibration, predefined metrics and uncertainty reporting. Same-model judgment is not independent acceptance. Candidate comparison: hidden-outcome tests across same-model, other-model and executable graders, recording cost and misses.
