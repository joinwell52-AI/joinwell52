# 2026-11-17 Agent Research and Competitive Intelligence

The highest-priority research questions are **preventing agent messages from impersonating human authority; distinguishing returned Stop/timeout from stopped effects; and distinguishing child output from delivery and acceptance by its parent.** Orca, Codex, OpenHands, Paperclip and ADK supply concrete fixes or open counterexamples. The AAE A2A-extension discussion adds request binding and accepted-but-failed versus rejected-and-not-invoked semantics, still an open proposal.

This run contains **44 observations → six analyses**. Execution started at **2026-11-17 10:48:23 Asia/Shanghai (02:48:23 UTC)**. Fresh main Registry contains 47 researchers. New observations continue per-source successful baselines, while historical pagination gaps remain pending. Event times below are UTC, with release and merge dates distinguished.

A merge establishes upstream mainline status, not packaged deployment or default enablement unless a Release or official availability announcement is stated. Test numbers are attributed upstream evidence. **No new local experiments, installations, articles or development-review assignments were completed in this monitoring run.** CodeFlowMu/FCoP relationships remain research judgments and unverified candidates.
