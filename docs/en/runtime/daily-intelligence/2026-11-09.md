# Agent Research & Competitor Intelligence · 2026-11-09

The central finding is a shift from parallel Agent calls toward responsive supervisors, durable background tasks, traceable authority and evidence-backed completion. Deep Agents' async reference deserves priority experience; Paperclip's native Runner advances delivery; Paradev's cloud-to-host access and Palmier's auto-approval direction need separate enterprise-governance assessment.

This report is archived under the actual execution date, **2026-11-09 (Asia/Shanghai)**, catching up since the last successful monitor without fabricating November 6–8 daily reports. Execution began at 09:21:57 Shanghai (01:21:57 UTC); most complete commit-query baselines were November 5 at 05:43:53 UTC, with per-source local successful records authoritative. Event dates are UTC from original sources unless stated otherwise. Orkas #193 was verified during execution after the initial cutoff and is labelled separately. OPEN→MERGED and main→release are delivery/status advances, not newly invented mechanisms.

There are **32 observations and 5 analyses**. This is source collection and classification: no local product experiments, articles, formal development reviews or actual comments were completed.

## Layer 2: research analysis

### A1 Responsive supervision and continuous background work now have comparable implementations

Evidence：O4、O6、O20、O29、O30、O31

Supported: Deep Agents separates task threads, durable job registries, steering/cancellation and approval-aware notifications; Cursor, Claude and Orca add distinct conversation, background-tool and mobile-control surfaces. They operate at different layers and do not establish a universal digital-employee protocol. Hypothesis: a responsive supervisor plus independent task identity and completion wake-up may inform CodeFlowMu after comparison with its Runtime. Prioritize async-deep-agents experience, checking notification wiring, version drift, licensing, worker capacity and cleanup, then Orca weak-network control. Costs include workers/threads, ordering, deduplication, post-cancel effects and retention. Map chiefly to Runtime/product. Discussion: authoritative jobs after compaction and queued results during approval.

### A2 Authority must survive resumption, host transitions and credential changes

Evidence：O2、O3、O5、O6、O8、O18、O19、O22、O25

Supported: Codex separates project trust/write permission and orders grant persistence; ADK exposes representation-sensitive approval; Paradev opens an explicit owner-cloud-to-host channel while Palmier removes approval friction. These opposing directions cannot all be called governance maturity. Hypothesis: checking subject, scope and revocation throughout delegation/resumption/cross-host calls matters for CodeFlowMu Runtime/adapters and enterprise policy, not automatically FCoP fields. Experiment candidates: resumed permission modes, post-revocation host calls, invalid read-only settings and Enum approvals. Benefits concern explicit authority; costs include policy versions, credentials, recovery compatibility and human blocking. Increase Paradev/Palmier scrutiny; assess Paperclip delivery maturity separately from governance fit.

### A3 Recovery and completion require terminal evidence; silence is not success

Evidence：O8、O9、O10、O13、O14、O17、O21、O23、O26、O28、O31

Supported: strict_resume, stale-owner settlement guards, held-call waits, final JSON-RPC flushing, nonzero exits, refusal to grade empty results, compaction error status and ACK/consumption distinctions all clarify state boundaries. Similar fixes support bounded cross-project observations, not comprehensive exactly-once claims. Hypothesis: execution, acceptance, user reporting and website publication should expose separate success/failure/unknown outcomes; local applicability still needs experiments. Candidates: final-record loss, empty tests, restart during approval and duplicate input. Benefits are recovery/accountability; costs include durable state, causal IDs, idempotency and compensation. Compare delivery evidence with FCoP contracts while leaving locks/scheduling/cleanup in Runtime.

### A4 Governance evidence expands to role declarations, skill versions and independent signing

Evidence：O1、O7、O9、O16、O24、O27
