## Normalized candidates and decisions

### Q-20260803-09 — Multi-horizon Digital Employee execution and evaluation

```yaml
candidate:
  id: Q-20260803-09
  normalized_question: How should a Digital Employee runtime manage concurrent long-horizon work, memory isolation, dependencies, reprioritization, and completion evidence across a workday?
  primary_object: paper_and_official_research_summary
  priority: P0
  status: Selected
  reason: The primary paper exposes a bounded architecture and evaluation problem directly relevant to Research Queue, Runtime, CodeFlowMu, and Digital Employee design.
  next_action: Invoke Skill 03 — Deep Reading on the paper, benchmark design, architecture components, ablations, and evaluation methodology.
```

The Microsoft Research publication reports Multi-Horizon Task Environments with dozens of interleaved tasks, identifies context saturation, memory interference, dependency complexity, and reprioritization overhead, and evaluates an architecture based on hierarchical planning, sub-agent isolation, tiered memory, and adaptive summarization. The source also reports that artifact-based evaluation and screenshot/action-log evaluation disagree materially. These are source claims to be checked during Reading, not accepted as Research Center conclusions at Queue time.

### Q-W003-03 — Human authority lifecycle node

S-20260803-02 and S-20260803-03 were normalized as additional evidence for the existing P0 object rather than creating duplicates.

The sources distinguish:

- a review convenience from a security boundary;
- confidence-based auto-application from held suggestions;
- rationale and intent records from permission enforcement;
- centrally managed policy from local client configuration.

The item remains `Selected`. Queue Stage does not advance it to Reading because the Research OS Engine already has a higher-priority Reading item and the authority object depends on the ownership baseline.
