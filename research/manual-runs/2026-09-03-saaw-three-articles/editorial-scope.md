- 图 02–04 保留原报告商业样本快照。图中的 54 项及 D4 成员不等于当前正文的 55 项；相关差异在原有图注中保留，不把历史图表当作更新后的统计。
- 第二篇保留模型、治理、收费与准入的讨论；第三篇保留 23 个公开项目、协议职责和产品建议。路线图与当前能力仍分开陈述。
- 中英正文维持同一研究范围、主要表格、评级、限制和图表对应关系。所有题图为作者确认的 AI 生成概念场景，屏幕数字不是研究数据或真实产品截图。
- 研究资料以原报告的 2026 年 8 月 30 日范围为基础。本次网站发布不表示重新安装测试全部产品，也不是独立产品认证。外部链接能访问，不等于其产品能力或报价已经重新核验。

## English

This change integrates the author's individually reviewed v5 manuscripts into the research website as three independent articles, each with Chinese and English editions. The manuscripts come from an author-supplied, edited comparative business report; they are not a new scheduled research-production batch.

Website integration changes listing titles and summaries, site metadata, cover components, language links, asset paths, caption format and source notes. It does not recalculate ratings or turn product proposals into delivered capabilities. The author approved the three covers and explicitly requested publication.

- Article 1 retains 55 entries: 54 original commercial samples and CodeFlowMu as a first-party engineering reference. The query-oriented Xiaodian AI application is assessed as D3. CodeFlowMu receives a bounded D4 assessment for software engineering, not D5. Laiye remains a D4 candidate.
- Figures 02–04 preserve the original commercial-sample snapshot. Their 54 entries and D4 membership are not the updated 55-entry table. Existing captions retain that distinction.
- Article 2 retains the model, governance, pricing and role-admission analysis. Article 3 retains 23 public projects, protocol responsibilities and product proposals, with roadmap claims separated from current capability.
- Chinese and English preserve the research scope, principal tables, ratings, limitations and figure correspondence. Covers are author-approved AI-generated conceptual scenes; screen numbers are neither research data nor actual product screenshots.
- The research basis remains the original report's August 30, 2026 scope. Website publication is not a fresh installation-based evaluation of every product or independent certification. Reachable external links do not establish renewed verification of product claims or prices.

Sources and claim boundaries remain in the articles. First-party engineering records, vendor materials, public code and research inference support different kinds of claims and must not substitute for one another.
