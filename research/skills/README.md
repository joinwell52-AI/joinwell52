## Skill 01 intelligence profiles

Skill 01 is a dispatcher. It executes three source-intelligence profiles before triage:

1. [Skill 01-P — AI Platform Change Intelligence](./profiles/01p-ai-platform-change-intelligence.md)
2. [Skill 01-G — GitHub Engineering Intelligence](./profiles/01g-github-engineering-intelligence.md)
3. [Skill 01-R — Published Research Intelligence](./profiles/01r-published-research-intelligence.md)

The profiles describe **where and how to discover evidence**. They are not research columns.

All three profiles serve the same three permanent research columns:

- Digital Employee（数字员工）
- Industry Architecture（行业架构）
- Open-source Engineering（开源工程）

```text
AI Platform Change Intelligence ─┐
GitHub Engineering Intelligence ─┼→ Unified Signal Pool → Skill 02 → Three Columns
Published Research Intelligence ─┘
```

## Operating rule

Research Runtime Queue must not begin with article generation. It must:

1. load the authoritative Research Intelligence Registry;
2. execute all intelligence profiles due for the day;
3. record source coverage, failures and inaccessible channels;
4. merge and deduplicate signals across the three pipelines;
5. assign exactly one primary column to every selected object;
6. submit Selected or No Selection for each of the three columns.

A publishing target must never force a weak signal into selection.

## Boundary

These skills govern Research Notes and their source intelligence. TMPA Publication remains maintained by its dedicated publication task and GitHub history.
