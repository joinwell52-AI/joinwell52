# Research Skills V1.0

Research Skills V1.0 defines the reusable capabilities used by the joinwell52 Research Operating System.

The skills are executed in order:

```text
Source Discovery
→ Research Triage
→ Deep Reading
→ Research Analysis
→ Research Writing
→ Research Visualization
→ Evidence & Citation
→ Publication Editing
→ GitHub Commit
```

## Operating rule

Research OS Daily and Research OS Weekly must follow these skills. They must not begin with article generation. They begin with source discovery, relevance review and evidence collection.

## Boundary

These skills govern Research Notes. TMPA Publication remains maintained by its dedicated publication task and GitHub history.
