# Skill 07 — Evidence & Citation

## Purpose

Make every important factual statement, comparison, table and diagram traceable to its source.

## Citation requirements

- Cite primary sources close to the supported statement.
- Preserve a complete References section at the end.
- Include title, publisher or organization, document type and stable URL.
- Record publication or update date when available.
- Link tables and diagrams to their source basis.
- Distinguish direct evidence from Research Center synthesis.

## Evidence labels

Use explicit language:

- `The official documentation states...`
- `The vendor reports...`
- `The paper evaluates...`
- `The repository implements...`
- `The Research Center infers...`
- `Available evidence does not establish...`

## Table evidence

A comparison table must identify whether each cell is based on:

- documented capability;
- visible implementation;
- reported result;
- Research Center interpretation;
- unknown or undisclosed information.

## Rules

- Never fabricate a source, date, metric or quotation.
- Do not cite a secondary article when a primary source is available.
- Do not use one citation to support a broader claim than the source establishes.
- Vendor performance claims must remain vendor claims unless independently validated.
- Broken or inaccessible references must be replaced or marked before publication.
