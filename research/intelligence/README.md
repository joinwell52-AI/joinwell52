# Research Intelligence System V1.0

## Definition

Research Intelligence System is the source-discovery layer of the Research Operating System.

It separates two dimensions:

```text
Source pipelines:
AI Platform Change Intelligence
GitHub Engineering Intelligence
Published Research Intelligence

Research columns:
Digital Employee
Industry Architecture
Open-source Engineering
```

The three source pipelines all serve the same three research columns.

## Authoritative files

```text
research/intelligence/REGISTRY.json
```

Defines platforms, official channels, GitHub organizations and repositories, research sources, evidence levels, topics and cross-pipeline rules.

```text
research/intelligence/runs/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD-intelligence.json
```

Records the daily coverage and output of all three pipelines.

```text
docs/.vitepress/generated/research-intelligence.json
```

Build-time website projection generated by `scripts/research-intelligence.mjs`.

## Runtime position

```text
09:00 Research Runtime Engine
↓
10:00 Research Runtime Queue
   ├─ Skill 01-P AI Platform Change Intelligence
   ├─ Skill 01-G GitHub Engineering Intelligence
   ├─ Skill 01-R Published Research Intelligence
   ├─ Cross-source deduplication
   └─ Skill 02 Three-Column Research Triage
↓
Three-column Daily Research Plan
```

## Completion rule

The Queue task cannot be reported as complete after the effective date unless:

1. all three intelligence pipelines have a terminal coverage result;
2. every due source is checked, explicitly inaccessible, or failed with a reason;
3. signals are deduplicated;
4. every selected object has one primary column;
5. all three columns have `Selected` or `No Selection`;
6. the daily plan and intelligence run agree.

## Trust boundary

- Official release notes and documentation establish platform facts.
- Official staff confirmation may corroborate a change.
- Community discussion is a lead unless reproduced or confirmed.
- Public official forums are checked by exact section, with separate official-notice and precision-section lanes.
- Forum home pages are navigation only and do not count toward coverage.
- Communities requiring authentication or membership are excluded from the active due list.
- GitHub Releases, merged maintainer changes, tests and security advisories establish engineering evidence.
- Papers, appendices, benchmarks, datasets and evaluation methods establish research evidence.
- Popularity, Stars, forum volume and vendor claims are not independent validation.
