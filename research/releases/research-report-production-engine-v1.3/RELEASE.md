## Operating boundary

The GitHub scheduler opens execution slots and initializes governed artifacts. Actual source reading, judgment, research writing, visualization, citation work, and editing are performed by the ChatGPT Digital Researcher worker.

A scheduled trigger is not completion evidence. Without worker execution, the task remains `Waiting`, `Blocked`, or `Failed`.
